Privacy Policy

How your data is collected, used, and protected

1. Information We Collect

We collect your Student ID and your GPS location at the moment of check-in. No other personal information is collected.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your GPS location is used only to verify that you are physically inside the campus during the attendance period. Your Student ID is used to identify you in the attendance record.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored securely in Firebase Realtime Database, which uses encryption in transit. Only authorized teachers and administrators have access to view attendance records.

4. Data Retention

Attendance records are retained for the duration of the academic term and are archived afterward. Location data is not stored longer than necessary for verification.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Data is used solely for academic attendance purposes.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to your attendance records or ask for corrections by contacting your teacher or the system administrator.
