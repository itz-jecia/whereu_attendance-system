How to Check In

Follow these steps to verify your attendance

1

Enter your Student ID

Type your official student ID number in the field on the Check In page.

2

Verify your location

Tap "Verify Location" and allow GPS access when your browser asks. Your phone will send your coordinates to the system.

3

Wait for confirmation

If you are inside the campus, you will be marked Present. If you are outside, your attendance will be rejected.

4

Stay on campus

Your location is checked again during the class period. If you leave the campus, your attendance may be cancelled.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if my GPS is off?

You will not be able to check in. Please enable GPS in your phone settings and allow location access in your browser.

What if I'm outside the campus?

Your attendance will be rejected. You must be physically inside the campus to check in.

Can I check in for a friend?

No. The system links each check-in to a specific student ID. Sharing IDs is a violation of the attendance policy.

What if I leave campus after checking in?

Your location is checked again during the class period. If you leave, your attendance may be flagged or cancelled.
